Who we are

White Pix Studio is a creative post-production company redefining the look and rhythm of modern advertising. Founded within the B&C Group in 2014. From brand films to integrated marketing, we blend a Chinese perspective with global wisdom to create deeply moving, contemporary visual stories.

What we do

We are a full-service post-production studio where design and technology seamlessly merge to bring stories to life. Guided by a Creative Director, every project combines production expertise, aesthetic sensitivity, and a deep respect for the narrative behind every single frame.

How we work

We keep things precise, efficient, and personalized. We bring together a core team of artists, designers, and technical experts, backed by a network of local and international freelancers, dedicated to crafting innovative, flexible, and emotionally resonant work.

With rigorous craftsmanship and a strong commitment to data security, WhitePixl seamlessly blends local excellence with a global vision, empowering brands to deliver their messages with clarity, confidence, and style.

Each project centers around a dedicated team, reinforced by a long-standing, trusted network of local and international freelancers who share our discipline and taste.
